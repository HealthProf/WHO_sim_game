Your role

Regional Director, WHO African Region

Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo · 47 member states

Starting position

The widest surveillance gap of any region, and a fund that covers roughly three weeks of surge operations at current burn.

Geopolitical context

Several member states have signalled they will not accept a travel-restriction framework they had no part in drafting.

Strategic priorities

Protect the surveillance-adjusted case estimate as the basis for allocation; avoid being scored on confirmed counts you know are wrong.

Time itself has a cost. Your Rt drifts upward on its own whenever no fresh containment decision has been scored for a while — not deciding is a decision.
